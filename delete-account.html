Speaksy Account Deletion

This page explains how Speaksy users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

Option 1: Delete from the Speaksy app

  1. Open Speaksy.
  2. Go to Profile and open Account & Security.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Complete the confirmation and password verification.
  5. Confirm the deletion request.

Option 2: Request deletion without the app

If you cannot access Speaksy, email us from your registered account email address.

Email: filmybhai3@gmail.com

Request Account Deletion

Include your registered Speaksy email address in your request. Never send your password or OTP.

Data deleted

After verifying a valid request, we will delete the associated Firebase account, profile information, learning progress and other associated personal data, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

Processing time

We aim to process valid deletion requests within 7 days and will contact you if additional verification is needed.
