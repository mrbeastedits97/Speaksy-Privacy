1. Introduction

Speaksy is an English-learning application designed to help users improve their English through lessons, vocabulary, translation and speaking practice. This policy explains how information is collected, used and shared.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information, including email address and Firebase user ID.
  • Learning progress, including XP, coins, streaks, completed lessons and chapters.
  • App interactions, settings and technical information.
  • Text entered into the Translator feature.
  • Voice input processed by the speech recognition service when using voice features.

Please avoid entering passwords, financial details or other sensitive personal information into the Translator.

3. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage user accounts.
  • To save and synchronize learning progress.
  • To provide lessons, translation and speaking practice.
  • To display advertisements.
  • To maintain app security and troubleshoot issues.

4. Firebase Services

Speaksy uses Google Firebase for account authentication and storing user profiles and learning progress. Information may include your email address, user ID and learning progress.

Google Privacy Policy

5. Advertising – Google AdMob

Speaksy uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including rewarded advertisements and other ad formats. Google and its advertising partners may process advertising identifiers, device information, IP addresses and ad interactions to provide and measure advertisements, subject to applicable settings and policies.

Google Advertising Information

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by service providers such as Google Firebase and Google AdMob to provide app features, authentication, storage and advertising services.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain account and learning information for as long as needed to provide Speaksy's services or as required for legitimate purposes.

Users can use the Delete Account feature in Speaksy, subject to the app's deletion process. Users can also request account and associated personal information deletion by contacting:

Email: filmybhai3@gmail.com

8. Data Security

We use services and reasonable measures intended to protect information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Speaksy's intended audience and age suitability are described in its Google Play Store listing. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in ways prohibited by applicable law. Contact us if you have privacy concerns.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features, services or legal requirements change. Updated versions will be published at this same URL.
